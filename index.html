Features

Everything your shop needs

Built for Uzbek and Russian-speaking shops. No app downloads, no complicated setup — just connect your Telegram bot and upload a catalog.

💬

Instant answers in any language

Understands and replies in Uzbek (Latin & Cyrillic) and Russian — even when customers mix both in the same message.

🔍

Smart catalog search (RAG)

Upload any CSV — products, prices, FAQs. The bot finds the most relevant entries with semantic search. Typos and script variations handled automatically.

🧑‍💼

Human escalation when it matters

Complaints, refunds, "talk to operator" — the bot knows when to hand off. You get an instant Telegram notification with the chat history.

🏪

One backend, many shops

Run multiple shops from one process. Each shop gets its own branded Telegram bot and isolated catalog. Add a new shop in minutes.

⚡

Manager panel inside Telegram

See all escalated chats, tap to open, type to reply — all from Telegram. No extra tools, no logins, no learning curve.

🔒

Self-hosted, data stays yours

No third-party cloud stores your catalog or customer chats. Runs on a free Oracle Cloud instance. Embeddings computed locally.

1

Anything in Uzbek, Russian, or a mix — the bot accepts all of it.
